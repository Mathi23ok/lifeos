How progress is calculated

SMART progress averages measurable goals. Each goal combines its recorded measures (current ÷ target) and checklist completion, equally when both exist. Goals with no measurable progress show “No measures”. Habit consistency uses eligible daily opportunities over the last seven Tehran dates, including today. Tasks include linked cards and linked goals’ checklist items, counted once per dimension. Completed Kanban cards or cards in Done / Complete / Completed lists count as done. Archived goals and plans are excluded. These are activity indicators, not a rating of your life.
